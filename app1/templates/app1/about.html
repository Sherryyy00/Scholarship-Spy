What we do

Study funding from official sources, in one place.

Finding scholarships usually means checking dozens of websites. Scholarship Spy collects listings from official sources, keeps them up to date, and helps you see which ones fit you best.

  • Official sources. Listings come from the EU's Erasmus Mundus Catalogue and from summaries of Chevening, Commonwealth and DAAD programmes.
  • Kept fresh. Each listing shows when it was last checked. Programmes that disappear from their source are hidden automatically.
  • Personal matches. Paste your personal statement and we rank scholarships by how closely their subject, level and description match what you wrote.

How matching works

You write

Paste a personal statement of at least 250 characters. Mention your subject, the level you want to study and where.

We compare

Your statement and saved interests are compared with every live scholarship's title, subject, degree, country and description.

You review

Results are ranked best match first and saved to your history, so you can come back to them later.

Application tips

Before you apply

Start early

Many scholarships close months before the course starts, often in the autumn of the year before. Note deadlines as soon as you find them.

Check eligibility

Read the nationality, work-experience and academic rules carefully. Applying for awards you're eligible for saves you time.

Tailor your statement

Show how the course fits your goals and, where asked, how you'll use it back home. A generic essay rarely stands out.

Apply officially

Always apply through the provider's own website. Genuine scholarships never ask you to pay a fee to a third party.

Scholarship Spy is an independent student project and is not affiliated with any scholarship provider. Information can change, so always confirm details on the official website. Erasmus Mundus data © European Union, reused under CC BY 4.0.
